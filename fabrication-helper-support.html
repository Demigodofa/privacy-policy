Fabrication Helper support is available for calculator workflow questions, reference lookup issues, paid-download store questions, and app troubleshooting.

Contact granitemfgllc@gmail.com and include your device type, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Related pages: App Page, Privacy Policy, and Terms and Safety Notes.

Paid download support

Fabrication Helper is planned as a one-time paid download. App Store purchases, refunds, taxes, receipts, and account-level purchase records are handled by Apple through the Apple Account that bought the app.

Using the tools

The app includes imperial-first fabrication calculators and reference tools for fractions, pipe size lookup, structural shapes, linear cut planning, bolt circle layout, and equal spacing layout.

Calculations and reference values are aids for layout and checking. Always verify drawings, supplier data, shop procedures, and governing standards before ordering, cutting, welding, or releasing work.

Privacy

The current release is local-first, does not require sign-in, does not create a Fabrication Helper account, and does not use a Fabrication Helper backend for calculator inputs or reference lookups.

See the full Privacy Policy for current data handling details.
